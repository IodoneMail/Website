Welcome to Iodone Postal Service

Iodone Postal Service is an open source courier like DHL. It provides services like API, POS, Warehousing and more. It is a open source project that aims to provide a reliable and efficient postal service for everyone.

Our Apps

  • Iodone - The main app for Iodone Postal Service.
  • POS - Point of Sale app for Iodone Postal Service.
  • Warehouse - Warehouse management app for Iodone Postal Service.
  • API - Link your warehouse to the Iodone API via your eCommerce provider.
  • Customs - All Iodone parcels get cleared through Iodone's own customs.
  • PrintMail - Print and send mail through the Iodone platform.
  • P. O boxes - Register for your P. O box at the nearest Iodone location, order an item to it and we will ship it to you when it arrives in your P. O box!
  • Telgraphs - Send physical mail with just a click!
  • Stamps - Get stamps from Iodone to ship items without any manual paperwork!
  • Iodone Mall - Shopping with an touch of Iodone!

Express Shipping

Get your packages delivered quickly and reliably with our express shipping options.

  • Same State: 1 hour
  • Domestic: 5 hours
  • International: 1 day (possible customs delay is not included)

ClickNShip

ClickNShip allows you to add an CDC to your warehouse or straight up make an factory + CDC.

Then you can make it so that your packages are given to the customer way faster.
